Top-up

    Rules

    1. Skip a row when Allotment Closed or Freesale Closed says Closed. Skipped rows stay visible in the audit list.
    2. Ask for a release reduction to 0 when Available is 0 and Total minus Sold is not 0. The stock is off sale because of a release.
    3. Ask for a top-up when Available is at or below the limit below, for every other open row.
    4. Requests stay generic and quote the availability. The hotel decides what to open and where.

    Wording

    Use {property} and {city}. Put **double asterisks** around words to bold them. Your Outlook signature is added by Outlook, so leave the signature empty unless you need it.
